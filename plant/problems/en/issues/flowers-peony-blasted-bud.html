A peony bud turns brown and shrivels before it opens

The bud in the photograph is still closed, but it has become a brown dry shell. Most of the leaves around it are still green.

Inspect the specimen

Press the brown bud. It is a hollow dry shell, not a small green bud that is still fleshy. Check whether the same stem also carries a bud that is coloring and about to open.

Check similar symptoms

A damaged daylily bud is short and swollen and may contain a larva when opened. It is not a dry brown peony bud. A rose that grows a new bud through its center does so after the flower has opened.

Choose a response

Snap off the brown bud at its stalk and bag it. Do not strip every leaf: most leaves in this photograph are still expanded and green.

Track the next change

Count later buds on the same clump separately: those that open, and those that dry brown again. If leaves develop round brown spots with rings, photograph those on their own. This bud photograph does not show them.

Host-specific check

Record this unopened brown bud only on peonies. Flowers of other plants that dry at the edges after they open belong with the dry-flower guide page.

Follow the stem below each failed peony bud. A firm green stem ending in one dry bud has a different outlook from a bud above a dark, soft stem lesion. In the second case inspect neighbouring shoots at soil level as well as the remaining flower buds.
