Home Safety

预防家中跌倒

Small changes like better lighting and clear walkways can reduce fall risk dramatically.

7 min read • Updated Feb 3, 2026

Reduce Trip Hazards

Clear clutter from walkways and secure loose rugs or cords. Keep frequently used items within easy reach.

  • Use non-slip mats in bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Keep stairs free of clutter and objects.

Improve Visibility

Bright, even lighting reduces missteps. Add night lights in hallways, bathrooms, and bedrooms.

Supportive Equipment

Install handrails on both sides of stairs and grab bars near the shower or toilet.

Tip: review footwear and use sturdy shoes with grip inside the home.
Source: CDC - Older Adult Falls
