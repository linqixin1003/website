Home Safety

Bases de lévacuation incendie

Plan two exits, keep alarms working, and practice so everyone knows what to do.

6 min read • Updated Feb 3, 2026

Plan Two Ways Out

Identify two exits from every room if possible. Keep keys and window tools accessible.

  • Choose a meeting spot outside the home.
  • Share the plan with everyone in the household.

Smoke Alarms Matter

Test alarms monthly and replace batteries at least once a year.

Tip: set a seasonal reminder in Still Alive to check alarms.

Practice the Drill

Run a quick drill twice a year so everyone can move quickly and calmly.

Source: American Red Cross - Home Fire Safety
