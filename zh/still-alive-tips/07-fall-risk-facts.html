跌倒风险事实与预防

Falls are common but many are preventable with the right habits and environment.

6 min read • Updated Feb 3, 2026

Why Falls Matter

Falls are a leading cause of injury, especially for older adults. The good news is that most risk factors can be managed.

Common Risk Factors

  • Muscle weakness or poor balance.
  • Cluttered environments and uneven lighting.
  • Medication side effects or vision issues.

Simple Prevention Steps

Combine home safety upgrades with regular strength and balance activities.

Tip: track balance exercises as a weekly habit and add a check-in reminder.
Source: WHO - Falls Fact Sheet
