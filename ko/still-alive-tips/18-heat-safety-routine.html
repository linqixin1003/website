Stay cool and hydrated during hot days and heat waves.

5 min read - Updated Feb 3, 2026

Plan for Heat

Shift errands to cooler hours and monitor local alerts.

  • Avoid peak afternoon heat
  • Wear light, breathable clothing

Cool Down

Use fans, shade, and cool showers to lower body temperature.

Check on Others

Reach out to neighbors or family who may be at higher risk.

Tip: keep a water bottle visible to remind yourself to drink often.
Source: CDC - Extreme Heat
