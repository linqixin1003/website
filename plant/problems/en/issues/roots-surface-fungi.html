Fungal growth on potting mix

Fungal growth or mushrooms on potting mix

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check whether growth is confined to the potting mix or reaches the base of the stem. Look at the condition of the newest leaves.

Possible causes and next steps

Fungi that break down organic matter can fruit in pots. Clear dead leaves and check how long the mix stays wet. Examine roots if the stem base softens.

How to tell similar problems apart

A mushroom or a patch of fungal threads on potting mix often feeds on decaying organic matter. Check the plant itself for wilting and the roots for soft tissue before attributing damage to the surface growth.

What to do now

Clear dead leaves from the pot, empty standing water from the saucer, and keep unidentified mushrooms away from children and pets.

What to check next

If foliage and roots remain healthy, monitor the moisture cycle. If the plant wilts while the mix stays wet, inspect the roots rather than judging by the number of mushrooms.
