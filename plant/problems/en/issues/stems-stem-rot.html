Stem rot

Soft, rotting stems

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check whether softening starts at the soil line, and look for discoloration, sunken tissue or injury.

Possible causes and next steps

Persistent moisture can damage the stem base. Pull wet mulch away, prevent water pooling at the crown and inspect the roots.
