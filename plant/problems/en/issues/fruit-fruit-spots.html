Fruit spotting

Dark spots on fruit

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Compare spot placement, sunken areas and other fruit on the same plant.

Possible causes and next steps

Abrasion, sun injury and infection may darken fruit. Track spot enlargement and avoid bruising healthy fruit.

How to tell similar problems apart

Look at the skin in side light. A shallow healed scrape, a sunken enlarging lesion and many tiny punctures suggest different problems even when all appear brown.

What to do now

Photograph an early spot beside a ruler. Compare exposed and shaded fruit and cut one damaged fruit to see whether discoloration extends into the flesh.

What to check next

Check the same fruit and neighboring young fruits after wet weather. Rapid expansion, softening or similar spots on leaves makes a local diagnostic review more useful.
