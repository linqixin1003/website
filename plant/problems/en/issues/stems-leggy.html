Insufficient light

Pale, stretched shoots

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Compare the distance between nodes on new and old shoots. Check for leaning toward light.

Possible causes and next steps

Low light can cause elongated growth. Improve light gradually within the species’ tolerance.

How to tell similar problems apart

Compare the spacing between successive leaves with older growth on the same shoot. Long gaps and a lean toward a window suggest light is limiting.

What to do now

Move the plant closer to suitable light gradually. Support weak stems while new growth develops.

What to check next

Old internodes will not shorten. Compare the spacing and firmness of the next several leaves.
