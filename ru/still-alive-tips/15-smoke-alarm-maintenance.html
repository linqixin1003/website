Обслуживание дымовых извещателей

Working alarms buy time. Test and replace them on a schedule.

4 min read - Updated Feb 3, 2026

Test Monthly

Press the test button and replace batteries as needed.

  • Test every month
  • Replace batteries yearly

Place Alarms Well

Install alarms on every level and near sleeping areas.

Replace Devices

Most alarms need replacement about every 10 years.

Tip: set a seasonal reminder to test alarms and replace batteries.
