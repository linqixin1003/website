Leafhoppers

Wedge-shaped jumping insects

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Disturb a leaf lightly and watch for sideways jumping. Photograph the body and faded areas of the leaf.

Possible causes and next steps

Leafhopper feeding can cause pale areas. Compare new growth before attributing damage to a visiting insect.
