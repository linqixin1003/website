Brown spot

Brown spots on stems

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Photograph the edge of a spot. Check whether the surface is flat, corky, sunken or cracked.

Possible causes and next steps

Normal corking, abrasions and lesions can look similar. Mark a spot and watch for expansion onto younger growth.
