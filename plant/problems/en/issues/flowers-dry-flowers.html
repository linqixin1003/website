Flower desiccation

Dry, brown flowers

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Is browning confined to older blooms? Check unopened buds and the leaves on the same stem.

Possible causes and next steps

Older blooms dry as they age. If unopened buds also dry, inspect the root ball for drought and check for hot air blowing on the plant.

How to tell similar problems apart

Older petals often dry after a bloom has opened normally. Compare them with unopened buds: papery buds that never open suggest stress before flowering.

What to do now

Check moisture in the root zone and see whether only flowers facing a heater, hot window or drying wind are affected. Remove spent blooms if seed is not wanted.

What to check next

Follow a newly formed bud through opening. If fresh buds repeatedly fail while soil moisture remains appropriate, inspect inside a damaged bud for insects or discolored tissue.
