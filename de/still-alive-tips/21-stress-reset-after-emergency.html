Stressabbau nach einem Notfall

Calm the nervous system and rebuild routines after a scare.

4 min read - Updated Feb 3, 2026

Grounding Steps

Try slow breathing, stretching, or a short walk to reset.

  • Inhale for four counts
  • Exhale for six counts

Rebuild Structure

Return to simple routines like meals and sleep schedules.

Reach Out

Share how you are feeling with trusted people.

Tip: set a gentle daily reminder to check in with yourself.
