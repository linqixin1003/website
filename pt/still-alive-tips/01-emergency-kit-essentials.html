Itens essenciais do kit de emergência

Build a 72-hour kit that keeps you safe, calm, and connected if normal routines are disrupted.

6 min read • Updated Feb 3, 2026

Core Supplies

Your kit should cover the basics for at least three days. Focus on items that keep you hydrated, nourished, and able to communicate.

  • Water: about 1 gallon per person per day.
  • Non-perishable food plus a manual can opener.
  • Flashlight, batteries, and a phone power bank.
  • First-aid kit and essential medications.
  • Copies of IDs, insurance cards, and key contacts.

Personalize for Your Routine

Add items that match your daily needs: medical devices, pet supplies, mobility aids, or comfort items that help reduce stress.

Store and Rotate

Keep the kit in a reachable spot and refresh food, water, and batteries every 6-12 months.

Tip: put a reminder in Still Alive to review your kit every season.
