Longhorn beetles

Longhorn beetles and holes in wood

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Inspect wood for holes, debris and declining branches. A visiting adult alone does not confirm larvae in the tree.

Possible causes and next steps

Have a local arborist assess the host and holes. Avoid moving suspect wood.

How to tell similar problems apart

An adult longhorn beetle can land on healthy wood. Fresh boring dust and repeated holes on a declining branch give stronger evidence of a borer.

What to do now

Photograph the hole, dust and surrounding bark. Avoid probing deep into the trunk or moving suspect firewood elsewhere.

What to check next

Seek an arborist if a large limb weakens or fresh dust keeps appearing. Retain a photo of any adult for identification.
