Galls

Raised galls on leaves

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check whether the swelling is part of the leaf and whether it corresponds to a mark on the other side.

Possible causes and next steps

Insects and mites can induce galls. Forms differ among hosts; identify the host and document new growth.
