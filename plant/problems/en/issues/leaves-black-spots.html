Black patches on leaves

The black patches sit in the leaf tissue and do not wipe off. A film that wipes off belongs with the sooty guide page, and tiny crowded dots belong with the black-speck guide page.

What to look for

Look for dark lesions within the leaf, yellowing around spots and premature loss of lower leaves.

Look at the green tissue between neighbouring black spots. On a rose, separate spots with feathery edges and yellowing around them are worth documenting before they merge. On other hosts, keep the diagnosis open.

Possible causes and next steps

Rose black spot can produce dark lesions and leaf drop. A coating that wipes off may instead be sooty mold. Remove fallen affected leaves, water the soil and allow air through the canopy.

How to tell similar problems apart

Separate black marks in the leaf from black surface deposits. Sooty residue can wipe onto a damp cloth; dead tissue does not.

Check whether each black area began as a distinct small spot. Several lesions at different stages suggest an ongoing process. Blackening that appeared all at once on exposed leaves after a cold night calls for checking shelter and temperature before removing healthy foliage.

What to do now

Remove fallen leaves beneath the plant. When photographing spots, include an early one and a fully developed one.

Use a nearby vein as a size reference. A uniformly dark old lesion offers fewer clues than the earliest discoloration at its actively changing border. If you request identification, include the plant name and both views of an early lesion while there is still green tissue around it.

What to check next

If affected leaves begin dropping rapidly, identify the plant and seek advice based on that host and the pattern of spread.

Trace a rose spot from edge to center

On rose, inspect several newly formed spots for feathery edges, then check whether older affected leaflets yellow and drop. A black leaf margin immediately after a frost follows a different pattern. Record the sequence before deciding that any black mark on a rose is black spot disease.
