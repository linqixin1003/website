Check-In

個人チェックインプランを作成する

Define who gets notified, how often you check in, and what happens if you miss one.

5 min read • Updated Feb 3, 2026

Pick the Right People

Choose 1-3 trusted contacts who will answer quickly and can take action. Make sure they agree to the plan and know your typical routine.

Set Your Cadence

Decide how often to check in based on your risk and lifestyle. Daily for higher risk, every other day for lower risk.

  • Set a consistent time window to reduce confusion.
  • Use reminders in Still Alive to stay on schedule.

Define Escalation Steps

Agree on what happens if you miss a check-in. Start with a call or text, then move to a visit or emergency services if needed.

Tip: write the escalation steps in a shared note so everyone follows the same process.

Share Key Information

Give contacts your address, medical details, and any entry instructions for your home. Store critical numbers in one place.
