Know when to go, what to grab, and where to meet before you have to leave.

5 min read - Updated Feb 3, 2026

Know Your Triggers

Sign up for local alerts and understand the evacuation zones in your area.

Grab-and-Go Items

Keep a ready bag with essentials so you can leave quickly.

  • IDs and insurance cards
  • Medications and chargers
  • Pet supplies and a leash

Reunification Plan

Pick a meeting point and share it with your trusted contacts.

Tip: keep a printed route map in your go-bag in case your phone dies.
