FoodSnap · Delete Data

Lingjue Tech · FoodSnap
You can delete some or all of your data without deleting your account.

How to delete data in the app

  1. Open the FoodSnap app
  2. Go to your Food Diary / Today log
  3. Open a food entry and tap Delete, or remove individual water / related records from their screens
  4. Confirm deletion — the selected record is removed while your account remains active
What gets deleted:
  • Individual food diary entries and related nutrition totals for those entries
  • Water intake records you choose to remove
  • Other logs you delete in-app

Account credentials and remaining profile settings are kept until you delete the account.

To delete your entire account, email lingjuetech@gmail.com or use in-app: Settings → Account Info → Delete Account.

Need help?
Email lingjuetech@gmail.com with your registered email and describe which data to remove. We will process requests within 7 business days.

FoodSnap · 删除数据

Lingjue Tech · FoodSnap（拍照记热量）
您可以删除部分或全部记录，而不必删除账号。

如何在应用内删除数据

  1. 打开 FoodSnap（拍照记热量） 应用
  2. 进入 饮食日记 / 今日记录
  3. 打开某条饮食记录并点击 删除；饮水等记录也可在对应页面单独删除
  4. 确认后仅删除所选内容，账号继续保留
会删除哪些数据：
  • 您选择删除的单条饮食记录及其对应营养汇总
  • 您选择删除的饮水等记录
  • 您在应用内主动移除的其他历史条目

账号与未删除的个人资料会继续保留，直到您删除整个账号。

若需删除整个账号，请发送邮件至 lingjuetech@gmail.com ，或在应用内：设置 → 账号信息 → 删除账号。

需要帮助？
请发送邮件至 lingjuetech@gmail.com， 附上注册邮箱并说明要删除的数据。我们将在 7 个工作日内处理。
