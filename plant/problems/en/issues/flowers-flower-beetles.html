Beetles on flowers

Beetles on flowers

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Look for beetles actually chewing petals. Record their shape and when they visit.

Possible causes and next steps

Some flower visitors feed on pollen without harming petals. Confirm active chewing before managing the insect.

How to tell similar problems apart

Watch for a hard-bodied beetle actually chewing the plant. A beetle resting on a flower may be visiting for pollen.

What to do now

Look at fresh feeding edges and the undersides of adjacent leaves. Note whether damage is confined to petals or also affects new leaves.

What to check next

Compare the next flush of leaves with a photo. If buds repeatedly fail to open, collect close-ups of both damage and beetle.
