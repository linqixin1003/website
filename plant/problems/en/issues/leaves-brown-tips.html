Leaf blight

Dry brown leaf tips

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check for brittle tips, widening margins and crusty mineral deposits around the pot rim.

Possible causes and next steps

Uneven moisture and salt buildup can injure tips. Review watering and feeding records and judge recovery from new leaves.
