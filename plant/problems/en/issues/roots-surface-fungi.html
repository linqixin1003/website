Fungal growth on potting mix

Fungal growth or mushrooms on potting mix

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check whether growth is confined to the potting mix or reaches the base of the stem. Look at the condition of the newest leaves.

Possible causes and next steps

Fungi that break down organic matter can fruit in pots. Clear dead leaves and check how long the mix stays wet. Examine roots if the stem base softens.
