Leaf blight

Dry brown leaf tips

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check for brittle tips, widening margins and crusty mineral deposits around the pot rim.

Possible causes and next steps

Uneven moisture and salt buildup can injure tips. Review watering and feeding records and judge recovery from new leaves.

How to tell similar problems apart

A crisp edge and a soft, dark edge are different clues. Check the potting mix below the surface and whether the leaf touches a cold window.

What to do now

Empty standing water from the saucer and check that drainage holes are open. If the root ball is dry instead, correct that before trimming the tips.

What to check next

Watch the border between healthy and damaged tissue. If soft areas expand while the mix remains wet, inspect roots for decay.
