Gray mold

Gray-brown fuzzy growth on stems

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check for growth near wounds, spent blooms or dead tissue. Test whether the stem is still firm.

Possible causes and next steps

Gray mold can develop in damp conditions. Remove spent blooms and dead leaves and reduce crowding.
