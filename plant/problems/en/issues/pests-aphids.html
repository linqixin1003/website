Aphids

Pear-shaped insects on shoots

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Check young shoots and buds for clusters, cast skins and sticky foliage.

Possible causes and next steps

These signs fit aphids. Gently rinse a small colony away and look for predators such as lady beetles.
