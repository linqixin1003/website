Black spot

Black patches on leaves

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Look for dark lesions within the leaf, yellowing around spots and premature loss of lower leaves.

Possible causes and next steps

Rose black spot can produce dark lesions and leaf drop. A coating that wipes off may instead be sooty mold. Remove fallen affected leaves, water the soil and allow air through the canopy.
