Brown spot

Brown patches on leaves

This is one possible explanation. Compare the signs below with those on your plant.

What to look for

Compare window-facing and sheltered leaves. Note whether patches follow the margin, lie between veins or occur at random.

Possible causes and next steps

Sun injury, moisture stress and leaf diseases can each leave brown patches. Review changes in light and photograph a healthy new leaf for comparison.
